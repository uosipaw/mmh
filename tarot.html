Since the 1400s, tarot has evolved from a simple playing card game, to a conduit between future and past, to a divination and occult staple, and now, 600 years later, is viewed as a course of study and self improvement tool, extending beyond spirituality and into science, psychology and wellness. As a mirror that reflects our truest selves, Tarot reading encourages growth through self examination and introspection for skeptics and psychics alike. It is no longer tethered to an identity confined within spirituality. The way you relate your reading to your life is a personal journey, and the cards are a tool to help you navigate it. There is no right or wrong way to interpret the cards, and the meanings are as unique as the people who read them.
